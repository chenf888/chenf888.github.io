I’m Feng Chen, a high school student from China who started coding with AI in high school. I build incremental games based on The Modding Tree engine, creating wonderful experiences.

Beyond game dev, I explore the dark edges of the internet, AI JavaScript animations, browser APIs, and creative UI design. My goal is to use AI to make things that people genuinely enjoy using.
